## Jam and team

- Theme: **Less is More, More is Worse**. More menu variety, capacity, inventory and voluntary online work can increase potential earnings while raising cognitive load, cost or risk. Less means easier execution but less income. Gameplay must express this, not just narration.
- 2D Indonesian restaurant typing / planning game, Godot 4, GDScript; Windows and Linux desktop mouse-and-keyboard target. 32×32-pixel tile assets. **Approved M5-A composition (2026-11-18, superseding the earlier 10×9 and M0 13×9 left-lane sketches):** a dedicated **left vertical UI region** (`LeftHUDRegion`), followed on the right by a **`12×9` (`32×32` px) restaurant world** with kitchen across upper rows (`cols 0..7, rows 0..3`), passive long counter (`row 4, cols 0..5`) + `online_station` `(6,4)` + passage `(7,4)`, dining area below (`cols 0..7, rows 5..8` with non-walkable driver pickup spots at `(7,5)..(7,7)`), interior wall + doorway (`col 8`), **right-side dine-in customer queue in column 9** (`4` waiting anchors), and **right-side road (`cols 10..11`)** hosting driver overflow. Each dining table (starter and dynamically purchased) has a visual-only south-side chair (`ChairVisual` + `SeatAnchor`) with table hitbox coverage; do not use the chair sprite as the tabletop sprite. See `WORLD_LAYOUT_AND_VIEWPORT.md`.
- 72-hour jam build, one developer using Claude Code is the sole code author. Coordinator can code but owns design/architecture and acceptance; two pixel artists, one UI artist. Art is in production; use labeled placeholders. No save/load required; one fresh run per game launch.
- All player-facing labels and typed words are **Indonesian** (technical docs may be English). Avoid employees, utility upgrade tiers, random perks, pathfinding, complex simulation, extraneous crafting, or a four-week mandatory campaign.

## Confirmed phases and controls

- **PLANNING:** choose daily menu from unlocked recipes, order ingredients to a **selected physical storage container**, buy/place or sell safely removable fixtures, review cash and start service explicitly. No kitchen retrieval/preparation before service. Fixtures including tables and equipment are bought/sold **only during planning**, installed/removed **immediately**. Resale is for less than original purchase price. *(2026-11-15 coordinator revision.)*
- **SERVING:** floor left-click teleports to clicked accessible tile, with **no automatic station interaction**. Clicking an interactable from any distance teleports to a legal **cardinal-adjacent** floor tile and **activates/selects it in the same click**; diagonal-only adjacency insufficient. Switching mouse ↔ keyboard and mouse travel is intentional pressure. Most work requires completing an Indonesian typed word; plates/work-table use mouse context actions. Clicking elsewhere cancels partial typed prefix.
- **Typing:** accept only characters extending a valid current-command prefix; invalid keys are ignored (UI shakes); full matching command **auto-executes, no Enter**; valid prefix gets ghost-text feedback. The station/context and selected target determine available words. B owns ONE parser; E displays it.
- **Automatic close:** service ends only when (a) no unspawned dine-in customers (`day_queue_remaining == 0`), (b) no outside dine-in waiters, (c) no diner in the restaurant (including eating), and (d) **no accepted online orders still open**. Dirty tables, unwashed plates and pending delivery shipments do **not** delay close. No manual Close Day button.
- **RESULTS:** freeze service; cancel and fully refund *pending undelivered* ingredient orders; erase all food and incomplete tasks outside fridges, empty box storage, restore all reusable plates and tables clean; retain raw fridge inventory; charge fixed daily electricity; settle service misses once; show results. Continue to next planning day. Cash **after** Day 7's electricity decides win (`cash >= target`) or loss. Win may optionally continue beyond Day 7; additional compulsory deadlines are not approved. Intentional bad investments may leave a run unwinnable.

## Kitchen / recipe rules

- Player holds **one item aggregate** (one loose ingredient OR an empty/part-filled plate with components). Dedicated kitchen work tables each stage **one item**; **dining tables never stage food**. Preparation may be interrupted and resumed. A serving dish must start with a clean plate and exactly match an authored recipe; prepared components may be added to that plate in **any order**. Wrong combination can be discarded at trash, not undone into ingredients.
- Launch *authored so far*: `nasi_telor` = cooked rice + cooked egg; `nasi_ayam` = cooked rice + cooked chicken + cooked tempe; `nasi_komplit` = cooked rice + cooked chicken + cooked tempe + chopped vegetables + cooked egg. Rice: wash then cook, then retrieve cooked rice from cooker. Chicken, beef, tempe, egg can cook; vegetables may chop, cook, or chop then cook. Cooking/prep completes immediately on typing, no cooking timers.
- **More recipes are planned**, with one new unlock intended per day across the seven-day run. Only the three above are **currently authored**. Build **data-driven** ingredient/recipe definitions and day-unlock entries so additional approved recipes can be added without code changes; DO NOT invent remaining dishes. Missing recipe content is an *asset/content intake* item, not permission to cap progression at three.
- Starting installed fixtures: **one box, one stove/hob, one washer (`wastafel`), one plate rack, one rice cooker, two dining tables with chairs**, plus map-provided kitchen work/prep table and trash. The same `wastafel` washes raw rice or dirty dishes depending on context. The existing work/prep table provides chopping (same physical fixture, separate interaction hotspot if needed). No separate purchased rice-washer/chopper required.
- Plate rack provides a **finite** configurable number of unique reusable plates; clicking rack takes one. On successful dine-in serve, unique plate follows customer → dirty table → `bersihkan` sends plate automatically to shared washer → `cuci` takes **one** clean plate into empty hand. Washer has unlimited dirty-plate holding capacity. Dine-in must wait **20s eating** after successful serve. Online success immediately returns the **same** reusable plate clean; no table/dishwashing.
- Storage is **physical per-slot**, not a distinct-type cap: **fridge 2 slots ×3 units each**, **box 3 slots ×5 units each**. Each slot holds one ingredient ID, duplicates across slots legal (one fridge can contain 6 `ayam`). Only *raw* ingredients store. Fridge stock survives days; all box stock expires overnight. A purchase is directed to a specifically chosen box/fridge and never spills to others. One retrieved ingredient per typed retrieval action.
- Planning ingredient orders arrive immediately. During service, ingredient purchases may be placed using storage's **Belanja** and arrive **100 service seconds later**, reserving capacity in the selected container. If still pending when actual automatic close starts, cancel and **refund the actual purchase amount**. Shopping/confirmation UI does **not pause** any service clocks. Fridges and rice cookers charge a **flat daily electricity fee** each; no metering/on-off mechanics.

## Dine-in, online, failures

- Dine-in spawn: first customer at service **t=0**, then a new customer every **20s**, until the day's fixed arrival quota is spawned. `day_queue_remaining` means **not yet spawned**; outside `waiting_queue` means **already spawned, waiting for clean seat**. Each dining table has one seat; outside wait is FIFO; 100-second patience starts at *spawn*, including while outside. Each customer orders **one dish from selected menu**, with cheaper dishes more likely. Wrong dish/table rejects without consuming it.
- Failed unserved customer leaves clean seat (no plate, no dish). Successful diner eats 20s, leaves table DIRTY and plate to wash. Tables must be cleaned before new admission.
- Online offers are visible at a physical **online acceptance station** and can be voluntarily accepted while eligible. Unaccepted offers are free of timer/penalty. On acceptance, create one physical **driver** bound to an order and start independent **60-second** deadline. Exactly **3 pickup spots**; extra drivers enter an outside-driver FIFO with their deadlines continuing. Only an in-spot driver is serveable: click **that driver**, teleport adjacent, type `saji`. **The online station accepts offers; it does not serve completed online food.** Online pays less, has no table/eating/dish cleaning. A missed *accepted* online order is penalized like a missed dine-in order.
- Online acceptance stops when **unspawned day queue reaches 2 or fewer**, even though previously accepted orders continue. Maximum **simultaneously OPEN** online orders equals **the initial dine-in quota for that day** (`day_queue_initial`), distinct from 3 physical spots; no total-per-day lifetime acceptance cap and no invented cooldown. *(2026-11-15 coordinator confirmation.)*
- Each missed order causes a **50/50** reduction of 1 or 2 expected customers on following days; D maintains persistent `demand_penalty_debt`, applied 1:1 against Planned Dine-in Quota after converting gross Potential Demand (`80%` floor). A following **perfect day** (zero actual missed accepted orders) restores 1 point of debt. No immediate game-over for one missed order. Demand base grows **+4 expected dine-in customers per successive day** (configurable as requested); active menu choice contributes diminishing Potential Demand (`[0, 4, 7, 8, 9]`), exact coefficients are TUNING.
